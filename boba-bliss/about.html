A small counter that grew into a habit

Boba Bliss started with one recipe worth repeating. Six years later, that's still the whole plan.

OUR STORY

Made fresh. Served happy.

Boba Bliss opened as a single walk-up counter in 2020, brewing tea in small batches so nothing ever sat too long. Word spread fast — mostly because the pearls were always chewy and the fruit was always real.

Today we run fifteen shops, but every one of them follows the same short list of rules: brew often, use real ingredients, and hand every cup over like it's for a friend.

MISSION & VISION

Why we make what we make

Our Mission
