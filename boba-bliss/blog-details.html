5 ways to customize your perfect boba

Small swaps in base, sweetness and toppings that change the whole drink.

Finding your perfect boba order is like discovering your personal coffee order—it takes a bit of experimentation, but once you find it, it becomes a daily ritual. Whether you're a long-time enthusiast or a curious beginner, the beauty of bubble tea lies in its endless customization.

1. Start with the Right Base

The foundation of any great boba drink is the tea itself. Classic Black Tea offers a robust, traditional flavor that pairs perfectly with milk. Jasmine Green Tea is lighter, floral, and incredibly refreshing, making it ideal for fruit teas. For a caffeine-free option, Taro or fresh fruit smoothies are excellent choices.

2. Dial in Your Sweetness

Not everyone has the same sweet tooth. That's why we offer customizable sweetness levels. If you prefer the natural bitterness of tea to shine through, try 25% or 50% sweetness. If you're treating yourself to a dessert-like experience, 100% is the way to go.

"The perfect boba is the one that perfectly matches your mood for the day."

3. The Ice Factor

Ice level doesn't just affect the temperature—it affects the concentration of your drink. Less ice means a slightly warmer, but much stronger tea flavor over time. Regular ice keeps your drink perfectly chilled from the first sip to the last chew.

4. Mix and Match Toppings

Tapioca pearls (boba) are the classic choice, but don't stop there! Brown sugar pearls offer a warm, caramel-like sweetness. Lychee jelly adds a fruity, refreshing bite. For a textural adventure, try mixing two different toppings!

5. Top it with Cream

A dollop of cheese foam or sea salt cream on top of your tea creates a beautiful contrast. The slightly savory, rich cream mixes with the sweet tea as you drink, creating a flavor profile that is utterly addictive.
