5 ways to customize your perfect boba

Small swaps in base, sweetness and toppings that change the whole drink.

When you walk into Boba Bliss, the menu is just the beginning. By adjusting your ice level, swapping the milk, or mixing up the toppings, you can turn a classic milk tea into your own signature drink. Here are some simple ways to customize your next order.

  • Play with sweetness: Don't be afraid to go 50% or even 25% sweet to let the tea notes shine.
  • Mix toppings: Combine chewy brown sugar pearls with popping fruit boba for a textural contrast!
  • Swap the base: Try oat milk for a creamier, nuttier finish.
Boba Culture·4 min read

Matcha vs milk tea: which one is for you?

A quick guide to picking your everyday order.

It's the ultimate showdown: the earthy, vibrant energy of a Matcha Latte versus the comforting, roasted sweetness of Classic Milk Tea. Which one should you choose?

If you're looking for an antioxidant boost and a slightly bitter, complex flavor profile, matcha is your best friend. Our stone-ground matcha pairs perfectly with oat milk. On the other hand, if you want something creamy, nostalgic, and indulgent, nothing beats our signature black milk tea.

Café News·3 min read

The story behind our brown sugar pearls

Why we still hand-cook the syrup every single morning.

Our brown sugar pearls are the heart and soul of Boba Bliss. Unlike mass-produced syrups, we believe in the traditional method of slow-cooking our tapioca pearls in rich, dark brown sugar until they are perfectly caramelized and delightfully chewy.

Every morning, our team spends over two hours simmering the pearls. This labor of love ensures that every time you take a sip of our Brown Sugar Cloud, you get that warm, molasses-like tiger stripe effect that makes it so irresistible.
