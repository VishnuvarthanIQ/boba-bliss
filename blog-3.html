The story behind our brown sugar pearls

Why we still hand-cook the syrup every single morning.

Our brown sugar pearls are the heart and soul of Boba Bliss. Unlike mass-produced syrups, we believe in the traditional method of slow-cooking our tapioca pearls in rich, dark brown sugar until they are perfectly caramelized and delightfully chewy.

Every morning, our team spends over two hours simmering the pearls. This labor of love ensures that every time you take a sip of our Brown Sugar Cloud, you get that warm, molasses-like tiger stripe effect that makes it so irresistible.
